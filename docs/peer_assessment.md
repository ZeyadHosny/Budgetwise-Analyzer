## This affects individual marks

§5: *"If the amount of individual contributions varies within a group, the
members of a group will be graded individually based on a peer-evaluation
form."*

It is the one deliverable that can separate one member's mark from another's.

## Requirements

- [ ] Every member listed by name
- [ ] Every member has a **written description** of their contribution
- [ ] Every member has a percentage
- [ ] Percentages **sum to exactly 100%**
- [ ] **Signed by all team members**
- [ ] Placed at the **end** of the report
- [ ] Excluded from the 10-page limit

## Two things the plan does not cover

`P5.4`'s completion criterion is only *"Contribution table sums to 100%."* The
assignment additionally requires:

1. **A written description per member**, not just a number.
2. **Signatures from all five members.**

Collecting and verifying signatures has no task in the workbook. See
`docs/requirements_checklist.md`, task 3.

## Guidance

`P5.4`'s note: *"Use actual contribution, not automatic equal split."*

An honest split is easier to defend than a uniform 20% that nobody believes. The
descriptions matter as much as the percentages — "built the amount parser and
currency handling, wrote the budget comparison" says more than "20%".

## Practical

Signatures need collecting before the report is finalised, not on submission
day. Digital signatures are acceptable if the course allows them — confirm with
the instructor rather than assuming.
